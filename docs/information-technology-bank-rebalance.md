Generated by `~/itbr/build.mjs` + `~/itbr/ledger.mjs` in the delivery session and checked into the
repo so the ledger cannot drift from the shipped banks. Every number below is re-derived from

## The rule

**Practice - build the floor, then the extras by weight.**
1. Exactly **100** questions at the end, and **every one of the 35 Information Technology lessons has at
   least one question** tagged to it - that floor is 35.
2. The remaining **65** extras follow module size by largest remainder: it-mod-1 21, it-mod-2 9,
   it-mod-3 4, it-mod-4 7, it-mod-5 7, it-mod-6 11, it-mod-7 6 (65).
3. Inside a module the extras go to the lessons that carry its examined core, including the lessons the
   coverage build added (the nine `fund-`, the two `net-`, the two `web-`, the two `wp-`, the two
   `sheet-`, the three `imp-` and the three `impl-` lessons); every lesson keeps at least one.

**Knowledge check - balanced at section level, keyed by lesson id.** Information Technology has **seven
module sections**, and `it-mod-6` splits across two of the five knowledge-check sections, so the grouping
cannot be built from module ids. Exactly **25**, exactly **5 per section**, and the five are five DISTINCT
lesson topics. 25 cannot drill all 35 lessons: the ten lessons with no knowledge-check item are named at
the end of this file.

seven module sections, and no module supplies more than 10 of a paper's 20 questions. The per-paper

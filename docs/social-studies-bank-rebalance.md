## The rule

**Practice - build the floor, then the extras by weight.**
1. Exactly **100** questions at the end, and **every one of the 24 Social Studies lessons has at least one question** tagged to it - that floor is 24.

3. Inside a module the extras go to the lessons that carry its examined core, including the newly added lessons (parenthood, parenting, the family laws, group interaction, culture, the four election lessons and the two governance lessons); every lesson keeps at least one.
